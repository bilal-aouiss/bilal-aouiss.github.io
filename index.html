16Scientific computing · Biomedical engineering

Asymmetric Flow & Oxygen Diffusion in Respiratory Systems

Studied respiratory fluid transport through numerical simulation. Solved the two-dimensional Stokes equations with finite differences in MATLAB, validated the solution against Poiseuille flow, and analyzed how asymmetric airway deformations affect pressure, velocity and flow rate. Complemented the flow analysis with stochastic random-walk simulations of oxygen diffusion near deformed and Koch-curve boundaries. Numerical flow validation showed a 2.08% difference from the theoretical flow rate.

MATLABFinite differencesStokes flowMonte Carlo
17Cryptography · Computational number theory

Diffie–Hellman Key Exchange: Classical vs. Polynomial Methods

Implemented and compared classical Diffie–Hellman key exchange with a polynomial formulation based on recurrence and composition identities. Used SageMath and Python to benchmark execution times across key sizes and profile polynomial generation, public-key computation and shared-secret evaluation. The experiments identified polynomial construction as the principal bottleneck, accounting for more than 90% of runtime in the measured configurations.

SageMathDiffie–HellmanNumber theoryBenchmarking
